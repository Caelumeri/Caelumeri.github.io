兰州大学化学化工学院博士研究生，天然产物化学全国重点实验室。我的研究兴趣从光驱动有机转化延伸到复杂功能分子的获取、结构改造和生物效应，希望把模糊的科学问题逐步转化成可以检验的证据。Doctoral researcher in organic chemistry at Lanzhou University, affiliated with the State Key Laboratory of Natural Product Chemistry. My interests connect photochemical synthesis with chemical access to functional molecules, structural perturbation, and biological response.

01 / ABOUT

关于我About me

实验、阅读和对话不断改变着我对研究的理解。我希望用清晰的问题、可验证的假设和扎实的证据，逐步建立自己的科学判断。Experiments, reading, and discussion keep reshaping how I think about science. I aim to develop clear questions, testable hypotheses, and evidence-based judgment.

从反应到功能From reactions to function

本科阶段主要接触光化学、反应器和有机合成；研究生阶段进一步参与功能高分子及其生物效应相关研究。随着研究继续，我越来越关注：能否通过对分子的获取和结构操控，解释复杂体系中的功能差异？My earlier work involved photochemistry, reaction engineering, and organic synthesis. Graduate research brought me closer to functional polymers and biological effects. My current interest is how chemical access and controlled molecular changes may explain functional differences in complex systems.

Organic chemistryChemical biologyEvidence-based research

“重要的不是把已有猜想讲圆，而是让真实结果改变下一步。”“The point is not to protect a good story, but to let the evidence change what comes next.”

02 / RESEARCH

研究方向与问题Research interests

在不同化学体系之间，我尝试保持一条共同主线：获得值得研究的分子，进行有信息量的结构变化，再追问功能为什么改变。Across different chemical systems, one question connects my interests: how can we access, perturb, and understand functionally important molecules?

FIELD / 01

化学获取与转化Chemical access & transformation

关注难以直接获取的天然或复杂来源功能分子，通过有机合成和化学转化构建可分离、可确认、可供后续研究的样品。Exploring synthetic and chemical transformation strategies for functional molecules that are difficult to obtain and characterize from complex sources.

博士阶段兴趣 · 探索中Current interest · developing
NATURAL PRODUCTS / REACTION DESIGN
FIELD / 02

结构与生物效应Structure & biological response

以官能团变化和可比较的结构扰动为工具，研究结构、化学性质、有效暴露及细胞或体内生物响应之间的关联。Using functional-group changes and controlled structural perturbation to investigate relationships between molecular properties, exposure, and biological responses.

既有研究基础 + 持续拓展Existing experience + extension
STRUCTURE–FUNCTION / TOXICOLOGY
FIELD / 03

光化学与反应选择性Photochemistry & selectivity

研究光驱动转化及催化体系的反应选择性，将反应条件、结构因素和机理证据联系起来。Exploring reaction selectivity in light-driven synthesis and catalysis, linking reaction conditions, molecular structure, and mechanistic evidence.

化学并不只是合成一个分子，也可以提供识别、干预和检验假设的工具。我希望继续学习如何选择真正关键的实验，而不是不断叠加技术。Chemistry is not just about making a molecule. It provides tools to recognize, perturb, and test hypotheses. I want to keep learning how to choose experiments that genuinely discriminate between explanations.
